Why offline-only?

Your books are personal. Primo Reader has no server, no account system, and no analytics SDK — the app makes no network requests in normal use. Your library, reading progress, and preferences live in a local database on your device and nowhere else.

There is nothing to download, nothing to subscribe to, and nothing that can break because a remote service went away.
